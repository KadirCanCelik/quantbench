Five colleagues—Alice, Bob, Carol, David, and Elena—sit in a straight row of five consecutive desks numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. We need to determine their exact seating arrangement based on four established constraints. First, Alice refuses to sit at either end of the row, which immediately restricts her to desks 2, 3, or 4. Second, David sits immediately to the right of Carol, meaning they must occupy adjacent desks in the strict sequence (Carol, David). Third, Elena is assigned to desk 5 on the far right edge. Since Elena occupies desk 5, the remaining available positions for the adjacent pair (Carol, David) are either (1, 2) or (3, 4). However, if Carol and David were at desks 3 and 4, Alice would be forced into either desk 1 or 2; but desk 1 is forbidden for Alice. Therefore, testing each remaining permutation reveals that only one unique configuration satisfies all initial premises simultaneously without contradiction.
